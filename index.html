Efficient and Long-Horizon Planning with JEPA World Models

Research Intern, Shanghai Academy of AI for Science · Jun 2026–Present

Studying and reproducing JEPA world models, from visual representations and latent dynamics to planning. I am investigating computational bottlenecks in MPC, exploring more efficient planning algorithms, and studying hierarchical models for prediction and planning at multiple temporal scales.

Building a Task-Specific JEPA World Model

Deep Learning, NYU · Yann LeCun · Apr–May 2025

code / presentation

Built a 3.63M-parameter, action-conditioned JEPA model on 2.5M offline trajectories in a two-room environment. Used Barlow Twins warmup, MSE alignment, and encoder freezing to prevent collapse, and evaluated generalization to new layouts and longer rollouts.

Ranked second overall and achieved the best model performance in the course competition.
